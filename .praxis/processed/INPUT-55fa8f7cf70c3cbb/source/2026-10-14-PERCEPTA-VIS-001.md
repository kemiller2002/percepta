## Original requirements and acceptance criteria from GitHub issue #36

## Research question
Does requiring an explicit product visual-intent decision plus valid token-contract guidance before coding reduce unsupported tokens, color/contrast defects, review corrections and downstream rework compared with today's agent guidance? Do not assume success; estimate effect and costs.

## Requirements
- **PERCEPTA-VIS-001.1:** Preregister a controlled comparison before data collection: control = current agent/requirements workflow; treatment = pinned Ordo visual-decision IR + published Forma contract/checks prior to implementation. Keep source baseline, requirements, task, worker/model, compute/resource budget, browser, package version and evaluator criteria comparable.
- **PERCEPTA-VIS-001.2:** Use representative UI tasks from Summa/Signal, including deliberately attractive-but-wrong hierarchy and dark-theme failure opportunities. Avoid contamination: freeze fixtures and evaluators; randomize order/assignment and blind reviewers to arm where feasible.
- **PERCEPTA-VIS-001.3:** Measure unsupported `--ef-*` references, unauthorized literals/fallbacks, WCAG contrast/state recognition, manual semantic/aesthetic ratings, human correction count, changed-file rework, accepted completion rate, token/cost/runtime, CI/browser wait, and decision/handoff overhead.
- **PERCEPTA-VIS-001.4:** Independently score machine-detectable correctness and human design judgment. High contrast never automatically equals appropriate palette. Report inter-rater agreement, failures and uncertainty.
- **PERCEPTA-VIS-001.5:** Publish a versioned protocol, source commit IDs, fixture manifests, raw/anonymized observations where permitted, and a conclusion distinguishing observed effect from speculation. Record negative/null results and the limits of small samples.
- **PERCEPTA-VIS-001.6:** Measure both first-attempt work and final accepted work, so the experiment detects shifted costs (more planning/checking but less downstream rework), not only fast completions.

## Acceptance criteria
- Preregistration includes hypotheses, primary/secondary metrics, stopping rule and invalidation criteria before workers run.
- Two independent evaluators or a justified equivalent for subjective aesthetics; objective tests reproducible.
- Reproduction instructions permit repeating with alternative workers and operating systems.
- Findings feed back into the [Visual Engineering standard](https://github.com/kemiller2002/visual-engineering/issues/49), [Ordo decision IR](https://github.com/kemiller2002/ordo/issues/92), [Forma token contract](https://github.com/kemiller2002/forma/issues/159), and enforcement gates, without declaring improvements absent evidence.
