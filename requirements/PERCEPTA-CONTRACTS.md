# Percepta semantic contract requirements

## Purpose

Percepta SHALL preserve product meaning when a user interface is implemented, modified, or regenerated by an agent.

Percepta SHALL constrain semantic outcomes without requiring a single visual layout.

## PCT-001 Contract identity

Every consequential screen contract SHALL have a stable identifier, purpose, and primary user question.

A contract SHALL be invalid when purpose or primary question is absent.

## PCT-002 Semantic regions

A screen contract SHALL declare the semantic regions required for the user to understand and operate the screen.

A region SHALL represent product meaning, not a specific HTML element, CSS class, card, column, or component.

Region identifiers SHALL be unique within a screen contract.

## PCT-003 Information hierarchy

Every declared region SHALL have an explicit semantic hierarchy.

Initial hierarchy levels SHALL include:

- Primary
- Secondary
- Tertiary
- Background

Percepta SHALL permit verification that primary information has not been moved into secondary navigation or otherwise made materially less discoverable.

## PCT-004 Visibility

Percepta SHALL support at least:

- always-visible regions;
- conditionally visible regions;
- collapsible regions;
- regions available through secondary navigation.

A blocking obligation or domain condition SHALL be representable as a visibility requirement.

## PCT-005 Domain-state projection

Percepta SHALL support mappings from domain predicates to required user-facing observations.

The domain predicate SHALL remain externally supplied. Percepta SHALL NOT decide domain truth or transition legality.

A state projection SHALL be able to require multiple perceivable outcomes.

## PCT-006 Capability projection

Percepta SHALL support contracts requiring that a domain capability:

- be exposed;
- be enabled when legal;
- be disabled when illegal;
- explain why it is unavailable;
- provide navigation to blocking information when applicable.

Percepta SHALL NOT grant a capability or make an illegal domain transition legal.

## PCT-007 Forbidden semantic patterns

Percepta SHALL support explicit forbidden-pattern declarations.

The initial catalog SHALL include the ability to represent:

- chat as the only authoritative primary interface;
- hiding blocking obligations;
- deleting falsified hypotheses or equivalent negative knowledge;
- color-only state communication;
- optimistic persistence success before confirmation;
- unsupported confidence percentages;
- forced linearization of inherently competing hypotheses.

The forbidden-pattern vocabulary SHALL be extensible without requiring a breaking change to the entire contract model.

## PCT-008 Responsive semantics

Percepta SHALL support breakpoint or viewport-class contracts.

A responsive contract SHALL specify which semantic observations must survive reflow.

Responsive verification SHALL evaluate preservation of meaning rather than pixel-identical geometry.

## PCT-009 Accessibility

Accessibility SHALL be a first-class verification category.

Percepta SHALL be able to require evidence for relevant concerns including:

- accessible names;
- semantic structure;
- keyboard operation;
- focus behavior;
- text scaling;
- reduced motion;
- contrast;
- non-color state communication.

## PCT-010 Verification evidence

Percepta SHALL represent verification obligations independently from verification results.

Initial evidence categories SHALL include:

- contract validation;
- structural verification;
- state-projection verification;
- interaction verification;
- accessibility verification;
- responsive verification;
- visual regression;
- semantic visual review;
- accepted deviation.

A verification result SHALL distinguish at least:

- Passed;
- Failed;
- Unavailable;
- NotApplicable;
- AcceptedDeviation.

Unavailable SHALL NOT be treated as Passed.

## PCT-011 Deterministic validation

Percepta.Core SHALL provide deterministic validation without requiring browser automation or an LLM.

Initial validation SHALL detect at least:

- missing screen purpose;
- missing primary question;
- absence of semantic regions;
- duplicate region identifiers;
- empty region purpose;
- invalid breakpoint ranges;
- duplicate verification identifiers;
- absence of required verification evidence.

## PCT-012 Dependency-light core

Percepta.Core SHALL remain independent of:

- Forma;
- Ordo;
- Praxis/ROS;
- Aegis;
- browser automation frameworks;
- DOM libraries;
- visual-regression libraries;
- LLM providers.

Framework-specific behavior SHALL live in integration or adapter layers.

## PCT-013 Echelon integration layer

Percepta.Foundation SHALL be permitted to integrate with the Echelon stack.

Percepta.Foundation SHALL reference Percepta.Core rather than duplicating its contract types.

Aegis SHALL be used for unexpected operational faults in the Echelon integration layer where applicable.

## PCT-014 Serialization

Percepta SHALL support a human-readable representation of contracts.

The serialized representation SHALL NOT silently become more authoritative than the typed model in applications that embed Percepta.Core.

Schema/version information SHALL be representable before Percepta claims serialized contracts are stable for interchange.

## PCT-015 Agent implementation guidance

Percepta SHALL be able to project a screen contract into implementation guidance suitable for an AI coding agent.

Generated guidance SHALL distinguish:

- required semantics;
- allowed implementation freedom;
- forbidden outcomes;
- required verification evidence.

Generated guidance SHALL NOT invent domain requirements absent from the source contract.

## PCT-016 Structural markers

Adapters MAY use implementation markers such as `data-percepta-region`.

Such markers SHALL be treated as verification hooks, not domain authority.

A contract SHALL remain expressible without requiring a particular DOM structure.

## PCT-017 Completion

Percepta SHALL produce verification evidence but SHALL NOT unilaterally determine repository work completion.

ROS, Ordo, or another governing application policy MAY use Percepta evidence as a completion gate.

A screen SHALL be able to remain incomplete when required Percepta evidence is missing.

## PCT-018 Contract drift

Percepta SHALL support traceability from application/domain concepts to affected screen contracts and verification obligations.

Future tooling SHOULD make it possible to identify potentially affected UI contracts when a referenced capability or domain predicate changes.

## PCT-019 Visual review role

Visual regression and vision-model semantic review SHALL be treated as supporting evidence.

Probabilistic visual review SHALL NOT be the sole proof that deterministic semantic requirements are satisfied.

## PCT-020 Independent implementation experiment

Percepta SHALL support an experiment in which multiple independent agents implement the same screen contract.

Success SHALL be based primarily on semantic contract compliance, not visual similarity between implementations.

A visually different implementation MAY be valid when it preserves required product meaning.

## PCT-021 First canonical scenario

The initial canonical scenario SHALL be the Indy Init Root-Cause Investigator Investigation Workspace.

The scenario SHALL exercise at least:

- competing hypotheses;
- falsified hypotheses;
- evidence;
- contradictions;
- unknowns;
- obligations;
- legal and illegal actions;
- persistence state;
- iPad portrait and landscape behavior.

## PCT-022 Evidence-driven evolution

When an implementation passes current formal checks but is judged to communicate materially incorrect product meaning, the discrepancy SHALL be treated as evidence of a missing or inadequate contract invariant.

Percepta requirements and contract vocabulary SHOULD evolve from such evidence rather than from arbitrary feature accumulation.


## PCT-023 Executable CLI

Percepta SHALL provide an executable repository CLI with at least:

- `compile`;
- `verify`;
- `status`;
- `doctor`.

The CLI SHALL return a non-zero exit code when a required verification obligation is Failed or Unavailable.

## PCT-024 Contract discovery

Percepta SHALL discover serialized contracts from `.percepta/contracts`.

When a command requires exactly one contract and multiple contracts are present, Percepta SHALL require explicit selection rather than guessing.

## PCT-025 Browser adapter isolation

Rendered-page verification SHALL live outside Percepta.Core.

The first browser adapter SHALL use Playwright and SHALL NOT introduce Playwright, DOM, browser, or LLM dependencies into Percepta.Core.

## PCT-026 Fixture-driven state projection

A rendered application MAY expose a test seam that accepts domain-state fixtures.

The test seam SHALL request application state and SHALL NOT grant Percepta authority to invent domain truth.

State-projection verification SHALL exercise declared predicates using explicit fixtures and SHALL report uncovered projections as Unavailable rather than Passed.

## PCT-027 Semantic implementation hooks

The browser adapter SHALL support stable verification hooks including:

- `data-percepta-region`;
- `data-percepta-observation`;
- `data-percepta-capability`;
- `data-percepta-unavailable-reason-for`;
- `data-percepta-blocker-link-for`.

Hooks SHALL be evidence instrumentation and SHALL NOT become domain authority.

## PCT-028 Machine-readable evidence report

A verification run SHALL be able to emit a versioned JSON evidence report.

The report SHALL contain:

- screen identifier;
- normalized-contract hash;
- generation time;
- completion result;
- every declared verification requirement;
- required/supporting classification;
- Passed/Failed/Unavailable/NotApplicable/AcceptedDeviation status;
- summary;
- evidence references;
- evidence source.

## PCT-029 Fail-closed completion

Required verification evidence SHALL fail closed.

A missing browser, missing fixture, missing adapter, unreadable evidence source, or other unavailable required verification capability SHALL NOT be treated as success.

Supporting evidence MAY be unavailable without blocking completion when governing policy declares it non-required.

## PCT-030 Responsive rendered verification

Percepta SHALL be able to drive each declared viewport size and verify required visible regions.

Responsive verification SHALL detect horizontal document overflow and SHALL capture viewport-specific evidence.

## PCT-031 Screenshot evidence and baselines

Percepta SHALL capture screenshots for declared viewports.

Percepta SHALL support establishing explicit baselines and comparing later captures against them.

The initial exact-baseline implementation SHALL be treated as supporting evidence unless a contract explicitly requires visual-regression evidence.

## PCT-032 Semantic visual review ingestion

Percepta SHALL support ingestion of semantic visual-review evidence produced outside the deterministic core.

External semantic review SHALL use the same evidence status vocabulary.

External semantic review SHALL NOT override or erase deterministic failures.

## PCT-033 Accessibility rendered checks

The browser adapter SHALL produce deterministic accessibility evidence for at least:

- document language;
- main landmark;
- top-level heading;
- duplicate DOM identifiers;
- image alternative text;
- accessible naming of visible interactive elements.

These checks are an initial floor and SHALL be extensible without moving accessibility authority into an LLM.

## PCT-034 Doctor

`percepta doctor` SHALL validate discovered contracts and SHALL prove that the configured browser automation runtime can launch.

A browser runtime that cannot launch SHALL make doctor fail.

## PCT-035 Canonical CI proof

CI SHALL prove the full Indy Init vertical slice by:

1. building Core, Foundation, adapter, and CLI;
2. running Core tests;
3. installing the Playwright-matched Chromium;
4. running doctor;
5. compiling the serialized contract;
6. demonstrating that missing required state fixtures fail closed;
7. establishing temporary visual baselines;
8. re-running verification against those baselines;
9. producing and retaining evidence artifacts.
