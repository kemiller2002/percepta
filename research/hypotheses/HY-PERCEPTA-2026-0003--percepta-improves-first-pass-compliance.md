# Hypothesis

Independent implementation sessions given the Percepta contract in addition to the same frozen product brief will achieve a higher first-pass required Percepta evidence pass rate than sessions given the product brief alone.

This pilot uses two sessions per condition and is intended to detect a directional signal, not establish a population effect.
